How to ride
• Hold PEDAL (right side) to speed up, BRAKE (left) to slow down; let go to coast.
• When the JUMP ring closes, tap JUMP: Perfect / Good / Miss.
• Berms: swipe ← or → to match the arrow.
• Tricks in the air (right after you jump): swipe ←/→ = WHIP, swipe ↓ = BACKFLIP. Big jumps fit combos (flip + whip on the Moonbooter!). Start too late = Sketchy landing.
• Keyboard: →/D pedal, ←/A brake, Space jump, J / L whip or berm left/right, K backflip.
